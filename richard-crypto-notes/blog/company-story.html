Who's Behind iCoinPro — A Quick Rundown

The founders, and where the company says it's headed next.

iCoinPro was co-founded by Paul De Sousa and Justin Clark. Justin built the Micro Profit System and the Tradefinder tool — the software side of the platform. Paul has focused on the education side, along with marketing and training, and previously served as CEO.

The company runs on two connected pieces: a training platform for learning crypto trading, and an affiliate program that pays members for referring others — structured as a unilevel/matrix compensation plan with monthly membership dues. That combination is worth understanding clearly before joining: some people come purely for the trading education, others are drawn by the referral income potential, and it's fair to say the company promotes both.

If you're evaluating this for yourself, it's worth reading both the training curriculum and the compensation plan before deciding which part (or both) is actually useful to you.
