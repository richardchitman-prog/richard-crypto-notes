What the Tradefinder Tool Actually Does

Field notes on the trade-scanning software built into iCoinPro's Micro Profit System.

One of the first things you run into inside iCoinPro's platform is the Tradefinder — a piece of software that scans the crypto market and surfaces setups that match certain patterns. It's not a magic signal generator, and it doesn't place trades for you. What it does is narrow down what you're looking at, so instead of scanning dozens of charts yourself, you're reviewing a shorter list the software already flagged.

That distinction matters. The tool finds candidates; you still decide whether a setup makes sense, and you're still the one taking on the risk of any trade you make. Any tool like this is only as useful as your understanding of what it's actually showing you — which is why the training modules spend real time on reading the output, not just clicking through the software.

If you're the kind of person who wants to understand a market before acting in it, rather than just following a signal blindly, this is the part of the platform worth spending the most time on early.
