Why I Tell You Upfront That I Get Paid for This

November 15, 2026

Before we go any further — I'm an affiliate for iCoinPro. If you click through from this blog and eventually sign up for something, I can earn from that. I'm not burying this in a footer somewhere. I want it said plainly, near the top, every time it's relevant.

I bring this up again (I know it's in my FAQ too) because I think it matters more than most disclosures people skim past. Crypto content is full of people who either pretend they have no stake in what they're recommending, or who are so deep in the stake that the honesty gets lost. I'd rather just tell you where I stand and let you weigh what I say accordingly.

What "affiliate" actually means here

iCoinPro pays people like me through a unilevel structure. I've written a separate post that breaks down how unilevel plans work mechanically, so I won't re-explain the whole thing here. The short version: if someone signs up through my link and continues on as a member, I can earn a commission tied to that relationship, and there are additional levels built into the structure beyond just direct referrals.

That's it. There's no mystery formula, no "secret system" I'm not telling you about. It's a standard compensation structure that a lot of membership and education platforms use. I'm not going to pretend it's more complicated than it is, and I'm not going to pretend it isn't there.

What this means for you as a reader

A few things I think are fair to point out, so you can read this blog the way you'd want to:

  • When I write about a tool or a feature inside iCoinPro, I'm describing something I use and get paid in connection with. I try to be accurate about what it does and doesn't do, but you should know my description isn't coming from a neutral third party.
  • I'm not going to tell you what you could earn, because I don't know, and anyone who tells you a number is guessing or selling. Income in anything tied to recruiting or referring other people depends entirely on what you do, who you talk to, and factors neither of us control.
  • I'm never going to pressure you toward a decision. No countdowns, no "limited spots," no framing this as something you'll regret missing. If this isn't for you, it isn't for you, and that's a completely fine outcome.

Why I keep saying it instead of saying it once

Honestly, it would be easier to disclose it once in a terms page and move on. But I think the people reading a blog like this are here because they're trying to learn something real about crypto, not because they're looking for a pitch. If I only disclosed the affiliate relationship in the places most people don't read, I'd be technically honest and practically misleading. I'd rather be redundantly honest than technically covered.

So: I write about charts, tools, and things I'm learning because I find them genuinely useful to think through in public. I also happen to be compensated through iCoinPro's affiliate program, structured as a unilevel plan, when people join through my links. Both of those things are true at the same time, and I think you deserve to know both before you decide what any of this is worth to you.
